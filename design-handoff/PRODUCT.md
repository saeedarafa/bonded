## Platform

adaptive

The app is Expo (React Native) for iOS and Android. The marketing site `trybonded.com` is a plain static site (web) on Cloudflare Pages.

## Users

Couples who are dating, living together or married, including long-distance couples. They want an easy, low-effort way to keep learning about each other and stay close through busy lives.

On the website the main visitor is a **curious couple** (founder, 2026-11-11): someone who heard of Bonded and is deciding whether to download it. A second visitor is the **invited partner** who opens an invite link (`/j/{code}`) without the app installed.

## Product Purpose

One small daily ritual. Both partners answer the same question each day. Each person sees their partner's answer only after giving their own. Once both have answered they talk about it in a short thread. It takes a few minutes a day and builds a habit of real conversation.

## Positioning

The lock: answers stay hidden until both have replied, then reveal together. Nobody can copy the other's answer or perform for it. The question is the same for both and changes at midnight in the couple's timezone, and it never repeats.

## Operating Context

- Pairing uses a 4-digit code shown as `BND·4827`, or an invite link `https://trybonded.com/j/BND4827?t=<token>`.
- Push notifications ("Sam answered, answer to see it") and a streak for days you both answered.
- The mascot pair Bao & Bun mirror the couple's day: waiting, together, celebrating, napping. They are never sad.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free: the daily question, pairing, threads, the streak, and History for today and yesterday.
- Premium: question packs, the full History, voice, longer answers, more little things. One subscription covers both partners. Yearly plan with a 7-day free trial, and a monthly plan.
- **Prices come only from RevenueCat offerings.** The website never shows a price.
- The website is static: no server code, no user data, no analytics on the invite token.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is **Bonded**. The mascots are **Bao** (the viewer, orange blush) and **Bun** (the partner, sky blush).
- Voice: warm, short, never guilt. Copy talks about being together and your bond.
- Original design and content only. Nothing copied from other couples apps.

## Product Principles

1. The ritual is the product: one question, two answers, one reveal.
2. Fairness: nobody sees first, so both answer honestly.
3. Warmth without pressure: missed days are naps, not failures.
4. Honesty in selling: no fake proof, no hidden prices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA text contrast (the `*Text` color shades on cream).
- Respect reduced motion: mascots switch to still poses.
- No gender field. Colors are relative to the viewer.
