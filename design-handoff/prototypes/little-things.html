Rules

  • Colours follow the viewerOn Maya's phone Maya is orange. On Sam's phone Sam is orange and Maya is sky, and the moment plays from Maya's side.
  • Hug back, at the same timeIf both send a hug within 10 seconds, both phones play "You hugged at the same time" with a bigger heart.
  • Sounds are short and kindUnder 1.5 seconds, original, bundled in the app (about 20 KB each, no download). The phone's silent switch always wins, and there's a Sound on/off button in the sheet.
  • NotificationsEach gesture has its own notification sound. At most one push per gesture per hour; extras still appear in the app. In quiet hours (23:00 to 07:00) they arrive silently.
  • Time of dayThe picker puts Goodnight first after 20:00 and Good morning first before 11:00. All five are always available.
  • Limits against spamUp to 5 kisses at once and a hug of up to 5 seconds. Per day, free: 10 kisses, 5 hugs, 3 miss-yous. Premium: 50 kisses, 30 hugs, 15 miss-yous. Everyone: one goodnight and one good morning a day, because that's what makes them special. When one runs out, its button turns grey; for free users the label says "More kisses with Premium" and opens the paywall. It never counts down or scolds. The limits live in the database, not only in the app.
  • One notification, not tenGestures sent within 10 minutes are grouped: "Maya sent you 3 kisses and a hug". Everything still plays in the app.
  • Reduce MotionEach moment becomes its final still pose with a fade. Sound and haptic still play.
