08a–hEmail sign-in · team draft for PO approval (D42, LEAD-09)

Replaces the placeholder email screens behind 08 and J4 (MOB-39 builds from this once approved). Tokens, type, spacing, Button, TextField, CodeInput pattern, Banner and the Bao & Bun moods are the existing ones from DESIGN.md: nothing new. Each screen is drawn light, then dark, on iPhone SE (375×667) and iPhone Pro Max (440×956), keyboard closed and open; 200% text for email and code. Copy is warm and never guilty; no email address or code appears in logs or analytics. The addresses and codes shown are test data. Full spec: specs/W-email-signin.md.

08a–fKeyboard rule and layout anatomy

Every email sign-in screen is built from four stacked parts. The system keyboard is a system element: it is drawn here only for its height (SE 260 pt, Pro Max 336 pt, Pixel 300 dp, each with the suggestion strip) and is never restyled. Visible area = screen − status bar − keyboard.

TopBar · back · progress (56)
Scroll content
mascot · title · body · FIELD · message
(shrinks, then scrolls)
Pinned actions
notice · primary button (56+) · links (44)
ends 8 pt above the keyboard
System keyboard
1 · Pinned actions never move under the keyboardThe primary button, Resend and Change-email links sit in a bar docked to the top edge of the keyboard (KeyboardAvoidingView on iOS, adjustResize + edge-to-edge insets on Android; never adjustPan). They animate with the keyboard (iOS keyboard notification, 250 ms), not after it.
2 · The focused field is always visibleOn focus, the scroll content scrolls the field (and its error line) into view above the pinned bar. Tapping a button never dismisses the keyboard first; taps use keyboardShouldPersistTaps="handled".
3 · What gives way, in orderMascot shrinks (96 → 48 on Pro Max, hidden on SE) · body copy collapses to one line (SE) · title scrolls out at 200% text · progress bar hides at 200%. Never: the field, its message, the buttons, the links.
4 · Notices ride the pinned barOffline, sent and link-failed notices sit just above the primary button, so they are on screen with the keyboard up. Field errors sit under the field.
5 · Closed vs openClosed: mascot 96 / 72, body copy, safe-area padding (38 / 20 pt) under the buttons. Open: 8 pt gap above the keyboard. The home indicator and the Android gesture bar are not covered by Bonded content.
6 · Verified by measuringQA checks, on SE and Pro Max, at 100% and 200% text: pinned bar bottom ≤ keyboard top, focused field inside the visible area (QA-52).
Drawn at 100% text. SE visible area with keyboard open: 667 − 20 − 260 = 387 pt.
