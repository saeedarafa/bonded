What we copy · what we fix

1One bright background across every shot, so the set reads as one brand in a scroll.
2An icon badge above each headline. Two-line bold headline, one idea, no subline.
3Real UI in a realistic device, cropped at the bottom so the screen feels large.
4Something breaks out of the frame for depth: a lock, a reaction, a widget.
5One frameless emotional shot with the mascot as the hero.
6In-App Events for featuring, and a description that follows the screenshot order.
ALead with our unique mechanic in the first three (the only ones visible in search).
BHonest pricing: free daily question, 7-day trial, one real offer. Their reviews punish the opposite.
CShip Android too. They're iPhone only, which leaves Google Play open.
DSocial proof only when real. Keep the slot, fill it after launch.

Our method · 8 steps

1Pick 3 jobs for frames 1–3: the ritual, the lock, the conversation.
2Story the 8 frames: promise → mechanic → payoff → feeling → habit → special days → content → price.
3Capture real screens from a "store mode" build: seeded couple Maya & Sam, 9:41, full battery, no real user data. iPhone 16 Pro Max simulator (440 × 956 pt) and a Pixel emulator (412 × 915 dp).
4Frame them: one background, icon badge, headline under 30 characters a line, device at about 86% width, one break-out element.
5Export at real size: iOS 1320 × 2868; Android 1080 × 1920 (Play caps the ratio at 2:1); feature graphic 1024 × 500.
6Write the listing to each limit, with the description in screenshot order.
7Plan a launch event: App Store In-App Event and Play promotional content, e.g. a 30-day couple challenge.
8Test and localize: A/B frame 1 (Product Page Optimization, Play listing experiments), then localize captions for top markets.
