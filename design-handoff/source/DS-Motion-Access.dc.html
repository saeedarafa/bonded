Reduced motion

1Mascots switch to still poses that keep the mood (lid on, snuggled).
2Confetti, bounces and pulses are removed. Badges fade in instead of popping.
3Hold to reveal becomes a tap, with a confirmation.
4Haptics stay on unless the system turns them off.

Touch and focus

Every tap target is at least 44 × 44pt, including icon buttons and reaction pills.
AFocus order follows the reading order: question, answers, actions.
BFocus ring: 2px you-text outline, 2px offset.
CNever color alone: locked answers show a lock icon and the word "Locked".
