Bonded: two of you,
one daily ritual.

BrainRot gives your attention a body that reacts to how you scroll. Bonded gives your relationship two: Bao (you, orange blush) and Bun (your partner, sky blush). They glow when you both show up, nap when you miss a day, and pop a heart at the reveal. They're never sad, only sleepy.
