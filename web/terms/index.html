2. What Bonded is

Bonded gives two partners the same question each day. Each of you answers on your own. Your answers stay locked until you have both answered, then they open together and you can talk about them. Bonded is a daily habit for couples. It is not therapy, counselling or medical advice, and it is not a crisis service. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number.

You can invite one partner with a link or a code. Invite only someone who wants to join you. Either of you can unpair at any time; after unpairing you can both read your shared history for 30 days, then it is deleted.
